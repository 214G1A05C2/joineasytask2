4. Core Workflows & Feature Implementation

Deep Dive into Verified Functional Requirements

👨‍🏫 4.1. Professor Assignment Management & Submission Tracking

Professors can create, edit, delete, search, filter, and sort assignments across all enrolled courses. Form validation guarantees non-empty titles, descriptions, deadlines, and legitimate OneDrive web URLs.

  • Dynamic Metrics: Displays "Submitted: X / Y, Pending: Z, Progress: %" with animated progress bars.
  • Individual Submission Audit: Lists every enrolled student with avatar, email, Submitted/Pending badge, and timestamp.
  • Group Submission Audit: Shows team names, assigned leader, member lists, and exact submission timestamps.
👨‍🎓 4.2. Student Submission & Acknowledgement System

Assignments feature status indicators, time-remaining counters, and direct OneDrive repository access.

  • Individual Submission: Clicking "Yes, I have submitted" opens a confirmation dialog. Confirmation records the exact timestamp, updates the UI immediately, displays "Submitted on [Date & Time]", and prevents duplicate submissions.
  • Group Leader Privileges: Only the group leader sees the "Yes, I have submitted (as Group Leader)" button. Submitting records the acknowledgement across all group members.
  • Group Member View: Non-leader members see "Acknowledged by [Leader Name] on [Date and Time]" without duplicate submit buttons.
Exact Requirement Verification: When a student views a group assignment without being part of any group, the system displays the mandatory notice:
"You are not part of any group. Create or join one to submit this assignment."
Along with the action button: "Create or Join Group" routing directly to the course group management screen.
👥 4.3. Group Management System

Enforces university group collaboration rules:

  • Single Group Rule: A student can belong to only one group per course.
  • Automatic Leadership: The creator automatically becomes the group leader.
  • Capacity Guard: Groups have a maximum member capacity (e.g. 4/4 Full). Full groups cannot accept more members.
  • Safe Leave Flow: Students can leave a group with safety confirmation; if a leader leaves, leadership is automatically passed to the next member.
